# Efir IPTV - Privacy Policy / Datenschutzerklärung / Политика конфиденциальности

Last updated / Stand / Обновлено: 13.11.2026

## English

Efir IPTV (package tv.denwag.iptvplayer) is a media player. It does not contain its own channels or content. You add your own playlist sources (M3U / Xtream / XMLTV).

Data collection: The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell personal data. There are no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs.

Data on your device: Your playlists, favorites, settings, PIN and recordings are stored only locally on your device. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app. Backup files are created only when you choose to export them.

Network access: The app connects only to the playlist, EPG, logo and stream addresses that you add or choose yourself. Those servers receive your IP address as in any normal internet connection. We have no control over these third-party servers; please check their privacy policies.

Feedback: If you use the feedback button, your own e-mail app opens with a prefilled message including basic device information. Nothing is sent unless you send the e-mail yourself.

Children: The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact: efiriptv@gmail.com
